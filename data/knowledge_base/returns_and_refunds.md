# Returns & Refunds Policy — Lumen & Co.

## Return window
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, with all tags attached.

## Non-returnable items
- Items marked **Final Sale** (including all clearance items)
- Personalized or custom-made products
- Gift cards

## How to start a return
Contact our support assistant with your order number and email address. Once approved, you receive a prepaid return label by email. Return shipping is free for defective or damaged items; for all other returns, a $5.95 label fee is deducted from the refund.

## Refund amounts and approval
- Refunds of $100 or less are approved automatically once the return request is accepted.
- Refunds above $100 are reviewed by a member of our team within 2 business days.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Banks typically take 5–10 business days to show the refund on your statement.

## Damaged or defective items
Report damaged or defective items within 7 days of delivery, ideally with a photo. We offer a free replacement or a full refund, including original shipping costs.

## Exchanges
We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order.
