# Shipping Policy — Lumen & Co.

## Processing time
Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Orders placed after 2 PM EST on Friday are processed the following Monday.

## Shipping options (United States)
| Method   | Delivery time        | Cost                                   |
|----------|----------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Standard | 3–7 business days    | $6.95, free on orders of $50 or more   |
| Express  | 1–3 business days    | $19.95                                 |

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union. Delivery takes 7–14 business days. Import duties and taxes are paid by the customer on delivery.

## Carriers
We ship with UPS, FedEx, USPS, and DHL. A tracking number is emailed as soon as the order ships.

## Delayed or lost packages
If a shipped order has not been delivered within 10 days of shipping, we treat it as potentially lost. Our support team opens a carrier investigation and, if the package is confirmed lost, sends a free replacement or a full refund — the customer chooses.

## Wrong address
Addresses can be changed only before the order ships. Once shipped, we cannot redirect the package; contact the carrier directly with your tracking number.
