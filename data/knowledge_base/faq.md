# Frequently Asked Questions — Lumen & Co.

## About us
Lumen & Co. is an online home and lifestyle store offering ceramics, candles, textiles, lighting, and furniture.

## Support hours
Our AI support assistant is available 24/7. Our human support team works Monday to Friday, 9 AM–6 PM EST, and replies to escalated tickets within 1 business day.

## How do I track my order?
Provide your order number and email address to the support assistant, or use the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email.

## Do I need an account to order?
No. Guest checkout is available. An account lets you see your order history and save addresses.

## How do I care for ceramics and stoneware?
Our mugs and vases are dishwasher safe on the top rack. Avoid sudden temperature changes to prevent cracking.

## How do I care for linen and wool?
Wash linen on a gentle cold cycle and air dry. Wool throws should be dry cleaned or hand washed in cold water.

## Do you offer gift wrapping?
Yes, gift wrapping is available at checkout for $4.95 per order.

## How do I contact a human?
Ask the support assistant to connect you with a person. It will open a ticket and our team will reply by email.
