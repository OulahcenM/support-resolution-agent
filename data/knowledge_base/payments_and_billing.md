# Payments & Billing — Lumen & Co.

## Accepted payment methods
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

## Currency
All prices are listed and charged in US dollars (USD).

## Charges
Your card is charged when the order is placed. If an order is cancelled, the charge is reversed in full.

## Duplicate or unrecognized charges
If you see a duplicate or unrecognized charge, contact support with the order number. Billing disputes are always handled by a human team member for your security.

## Discount codes
Only one discount code can be used per order. Codes cannot be applied after an order has been placed and cannot be combined with clearance pricing.

## Security
Our support team will never ask for your full card number, CVV, or password.
